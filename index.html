What is a KakoBuy spreadsheet?

A KakoBuy spreadsheet is a product shortlist that helps you compare listings before placing an order through a shopping agent. This independent guide pairs a sample of the CAFANS catalog with checks for sizing, warehouse photos, and shipping quotes.

How to use the shortlist

  1. Match the exact variant

    Search the title on CAFANS. Open the product detail page and confirm the seller, color, size option, and current price. Compare garment measurements with a piece that already fits you.

  2. Review warehouse photos

    If your agent provides quality-control photos, compare the size label, color, and visible condition with the variant you ordered. Ask for a close-up or measurement if you need one before approving shipment.

  3. Compare shipping on the same basis

    Use the same destination, parcel contents, and currency for both quotes. Check each line’s billable weight and ask which fees and taxes are already included before entering the totals below.

Compare two delivered-cost estimates

Enter both quotes in USD. Include domestic delivery, agent fees, taxes, and duties in “Other costs” where applicable. Enter each cost once. Leave a field blank to treat it as $0 in the estimate.

KakoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Does the catalog reflect live stock and prices?

The catalog is a sample of published CAFANS listings and estimated prices. Open the current listing to verify stock, price, and variants before ordering.

What goes in the downloadable shortlist?

Each selected item is saved as a CSV row with its title, category, estimated USD price, catalog source, and blank columns for your preferred variant and notes.

Can I use the catalog with a different shopping agent?

Check the agent choices on the CAFANS product page. Confirm that your chosen agent accepts the seller’s listing and offers a suitable shipping line to your destination.

Why can the billed shipping weight differ from the scale weight?

Some shipping lines calculate a volumetric weight from the packaged dimensions and use it when it exceeds the actual weight. Ask the agent for the packed dimensions and the rule used by the specific line you are comparing.
